Stock Market Forecasting & Decision Support System
Stock Market Forecasting & Decision Support System

A 3-phase production platform — not a simple predictor. Combines technical analysis, NLP news sentiment, probabilistic forecasting, backtesting, portfolio optimization, and a regime-aware decision engine that outputs BUY/HOLD/SELL with entry zone, stop loss, and take profit levels.

Arch
    FastAPI · GMM · GARCH · ARIMA
Scale
    3 phases, 6 strategies
Deploy
    AWS EC2 + Streamlit

    Phase 1: RSI, MACD, Bollinger Bands, OBV, earnings analysis, news sentiment (VADER).
    Phase 2: 6-strategy backtester with Sharpe/Sortino/MaxDD + portfolio optimizer (efficient frontier).
    Phase 3: Regime detection (GaussianMixture), EWMA-GARCH + ARIMA Monte Carlo forecast, global macro sentiment from Reuters/BBC/AP/CNBC.
